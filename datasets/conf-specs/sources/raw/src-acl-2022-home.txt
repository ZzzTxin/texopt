 Welcome Message 

 ​ 

 60th Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics 

 Welcome everyone to ACL 2022, the 60th meeting of ACL that will take place 22nd - 27th May 2022. 

 We plan to be a hybrid meeting, with as many as possible in Dublin!

In close coordination with NAACL 2022, we have decided to help implement the vision of using the ACL Rolling Review for major conference submissions and have worked out a coordinated submission plan to allow for maximum flexibility for the authors. Please check the ACL 2022 Chairs Blog and the soon to be released First Call for Papers for more details. 

 ​​​​​ With best wishes, 

 ACL 2022 Organising Committee
